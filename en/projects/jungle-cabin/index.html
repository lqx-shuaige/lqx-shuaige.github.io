CAT 04 / 11 · Web 3D

A cabin in the canopy

A warm-lit cabin beside a rainforest stream, with day and night, optional mist, and an interior you can walk into.

Archived Web 3Dthree.jsPrompt

Orbit the scene, follow a visitor in third person, or walk in first person. Take the stone path and wooden steps to explore the living room, desk and bed. Touch controls use a joystick for movement and dragging to look around. The scene and its dependencies are bundled into one offline HTML file. The original Chinese creation prompt is included below to read or copy.

Visit the cabin

Use Chrome or Edge with WebGL 2. Scene controls are in Chinese: WASD to move, V to switch views. Open “?” in the top right to select the lower rendering quality if needed.
