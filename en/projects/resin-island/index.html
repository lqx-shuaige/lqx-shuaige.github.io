CAT 02 / 15 · Web 3D

A resin island

Cliffs, a crescent beach and turquoise water inside a transparent resin miniature. Move across the water to stir ripples and a gentle wobble.

Live Web 3DReact Three Fiber

Inspired by the supplied resin miniature reference, the cliffs, plants, beach and seabed are built in code. A GPU height field drives ripples and a spring simulation gives the block a soft wobble. Adjust water color, waves, ripples, sun angle, tide, stiffness and depth of field. Built with React Three Fiber, drei and post-processing, bundled into one HTML file. The original Chinese brief is preserved below; the project cover is rendered from the actual scene.

Explore the island

Requires WebGL 2. Drag to orbit and move across the water to make ripples. Performance is still being optimized; select Eco for lower rendering quality.
